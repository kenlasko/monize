# Signed net spending for an expense category

For an existing expense category, the Spending by Category report shows the negative of the signed sum of its eligible postings in the report currency. Keep a group that has qualifying activity even if its net expense is zero or negative. A category with a purchase of 100 and a classified refund of 150 has net spending of −50. A purchase and full refund leave a visible zero group. A category with no activity has no row.

Use the category already assigned to the posting. Preserve the report’s existing date, account, tag, transfer, investment, currency and completeness rules. The result and its consumers must preserve the sign of the amounts, including the headline. A chart that cannot express the sign must expose the signed amounts accessibly. This change concerns an existing net-spending measure. It leaves uncategorized activity, purchase-only measures, their rankings and the ledger’s classifications under their existing definitions.

Monthly Spending Trend, the assistant’s category breakdown and period totals, and AI insights use the same classification. A month with a negative expense baseline has no percentage growth in Monthly Comparison. The change in money remains available. Month-to-date trivia compares two positive spending figures. A refund after the last purchase can still qualify for a no-spend streak.

The top and ranked category trivia sentences state a percentage as well as the category name and amount. They require a complete positive composition with a defined share. When refunds make shares unavailable, the signed table still shows the category and amount.
