# v1.20.0

Warranties, shared expenses, yearly caps on payroll deductions, SimpleFIN bank sync, converting a transaction into a transfer and back, and Microsoft Money 97-99 import. Every screen also went through a UI consistency pass, and two code audits fixed a long list of security and financial-math issues.

## Warranties

**Tools -> Warranties** keeps track of what you bought, when its coverage ends, who sold it, its serial and model numbers, its documents and any claims, and reminds you before it expires. It never changes a balance.

- **Three ways to add one:** **Add warranty** on the page; **Import CSV** (Warracker's column format); or **Add warranty** on a transaction, including one you are still creating in New Transaction. Coverage ends **On a date**, **After a duration**, or **Never**.
- **The list** has status cards, search, sort, archive and CSV export. Expiry is shown in years and days. A warranty's page holds the details with the vendor's logo, the purchase (edit it in place or **View in register**), documents with a type for each, and a claims log.
- **In the register:** a shield badge shows how many warranties a row has, the row's menu has **Add warranty** and **View warranties**, and `has:warranty` finds them.
- **Reminders** 30 days and 7 days before expiry and on the day itself, under a new **Warranties** notification category. A **Warranties expiring soon** dashboard widget is off by default; add it from Customize.
- **Shared access:** a new read-only **Warranties** section for delegates, off until you grant it. The AI assistant and MCP get a read-only `list_warranties` tool.

## Shared Expenses

**Tools -> Shared Expenses** tracks money you spend with other people: a trip, a shared apartment, a club. Nothing here touches your own accounts, balances, budgets or reports.

- **Groups** have a fixed set of people, and each expense can involve any of them. A person's share can be a fixed amount, an explicit zero, a percentage, or an equal split of the remainder. Nobody needs a Monize account to be listed.
- **Balances** show each person's position and suggest the fewest repayments that settle the group. Record repayments as they happen.
- **Foreign currencies:** an expense is converted at the rate you agreed or the market rate on its date. If there is no rate yet, the group says so and shows your known position until the rate arrives.
- **Files, Activity and Export CSV** for each group. **Invite** a person who has an account on the same server so they can see the group and add expenses themselves.
- Shared Expenses belongs to your own profile, so delegates do not see it.

## Payroll Deductions

A new **Tools -> Taxes** page, with **Payroll Deductions** as its first tool. It puts a yearly maximum on a deduction line of your paycheque's scheduled bill (a pension or employment-insurance contribution, for example).

- **A rule** names the payroll bill, one of its deduction categories, the yearly maximum and the reset date. The form suggests the usual per-paycheque amount and the maximum from last year's paycheques.
- **When the paycheque posts**, each deduction with a rule is capped to whatever room is left under its maximum, down to zero, and the paycheque total is recalculated. Two paycheques that deduct into the same category share one maximum. Posted transactions are never changed.
- **The Cash Flow Forecast** shows the capped deductions ahead of time, and Edit Occurrence opens with the same figures.
- **Paid this period** and **Previous periods** tables on the page, plus a reminder two months before each reset.
- **Payroll Deductions report** under Reports -> Tax: a yearly summary, every paycheque, and one table per category across the years, all exportable to CSV.
- Before you start, set your paycheque up as a scheduled deposit with one line for gross pay and one negative line for each deduction.

## Bank sync

- **SimpleFIN Bridge** is a second provider beside Enable Banking. In Settings -> Bank Sync, choose **SimpleFIN**, connect your banks in SimpleFIN Bridge, paste the one-time setup token, review the first import and turn on daily sync. Credentials are stored encrypted, and the card shows how many requests you have left. Investment holdings and pending transactions are not synced yet.
- **A payee chosen by an import rule brings its default category.** When a rule turns "CARD 1234 STARBUCKS #55" into your "Starbucks" payee and no rule sets a category, the row gets Starbucks' default category.
- **Fixed:** a bank that reuses a transaction reference no longer causes a duplicate or a skipped row.

## Transactions and transfers

- **Convert a transaction into a transfer, or a transfer back into a transaction**, from the edit form. The row keeps its attachments, warranties, tags and history; only the other side of the transfer is added or removed. Split transactions convert too. A cleared or reconciled counterpart gets a warning, and conversion is blocked when **Lock reconciled transactions** is on.
- **Transfers between currencies:** the amount labels name each currency ("Transfer Amount (CAD)", "Amount Received (USD)"), and typing one amount fills in the other at that day's rate. A figure you typed yourself, such as a received amount after the bank's FX fee, is never overwritten.
- **Calendar views:** the Table / Calendar switch now sits at the left of the toolbar on Transactions and Bills & Deposits, the month is centred, and clicking it opens a month picker you can type into. On a phone everything fits on one line.
- **Phone register:** the date toggle sits in the same row as the sort chips, and the Description and Ref # chips are gone because the card doesn't show those columns.
- **Undo** is disabled, with the reason shown, after the server refuses an undo, until something new happens.
- **Fixed:** switching the edit form to Transfer and back no longer flips a negative amount to positive. A cross-currency transfer opened from its receiving side no longer saves at a 1:1 rate. Amounts with four decimals keep all four.

## Reconciliation and accounts

- **Reconcile:** the summary bar stays visible while you scroll. **Statement Ending Balance** is pre-filled with the account's balance on the statement date. Every row on or before that date starts ticked, whatever its status. Outstanding transactions dated *after* the statement date are listed too (unticked), for a bank that posts earlier than your register does. The account picker lists favourites first, then A-Z.
- **Accounts page:** new **Last Reconciled** and **Unreconciled** columns. Balance now stays in view on narrower screens because the other columns give way first, and an investment account shows its Investments and Cash amounts on separate lines.

## Bills & Deposits

- **Hide the Cash Flow Forecast chart**, the same way as the chart on Transactions.
- **Reference numbers are saved** on a schedule and carried onto the transaction it posts. Clearing a schedule's description now saves too.
- **Month-based schedules keep their day:** a bill started on the 31st goes 31 Jan -> 28 Feb -> 31 Mar instead of staying on the 28th. Schedules that already drifted stay where they are.
- **Same filter panel and toolbar as Transactions:** All / Bills / Deposits moved into the filter panel, and **Export** writes the filtered list to CSV.

## Budgets, reports and dashboard

- **Remove a category from an existing budget** from its edit dialog. The allocation goes back to the unallocated pool. The category's history in past budget periods is deleted with it; your transactions are not affected.
- **Tagged transfers as funding:** if you tag the transfers into a shared account (for example `scope:household`), Income vs Expenses and Cash Flow can include that money as available funds without counting it as income or spending. Both reports get an account filter and an **Include tagged transfers** switch, which shows Balance and Balance % instead of Savings. Income by Source, Spending by Category, Monthly Breakdown and Budget vs Actual show the same **Available funds**.
- **Partial figures are labelled, not hidden:** net worth, account values, portfolio gain and the sector, country and asset-class weightings say when a holding has no price or no exchange rate, and name it, instead of showing a total that quietly leaves it out.
- **Dashboard:** the Expenses by Category legend shows each category's total. The Assets vs Liabilities legend is compact again. The Account Balances chart no longer cuts off the "$" on its axis. The redundant "View all" buttons are gone, since each widget's heading already links to its page.

## Investments

- **Return of Capital** is a new investment action. Its cash comes in like a dividend, but it lowers the cost basis instead of counting as income; anything beyond the remaining basis is a capital gain. Microsoft Money's `RtrnCap` rows (QIF and .mny) now import as Return of Capital instead of Dividend.
- **Sort Holdings by Account by any column.** The default is market value, largest first, and accounts are listed alphabetically.
- **A faster Investments page:** working out daily portfolio values is about three times faster, which fixes the timeouts on portfolios with decades of history. The summary sections now draw once instead of twice.
- **Realized and capital gains:** a sale of shares that were added with no cost shows its gain as unknown, with the known part beside it. A cost basis in a different currency is converted instead of being added as if it were the same currency.
- **Fixed:** in the evening, the 1W and 1M portfolio charts no longer start a day late.

## Import

- **Microsoft Money 97, 98 and 99** `.mny` files import directly, including scheduled bills, Money 99 interest, CDs and recorded splits.
- **Money classifications** become `key:value` tags, such as `Properties:20 Spring Apartment`.
- **Multi-level categories:** `Housing:Mortgage:Interest` creates every level in QIF, CSV and OFX imports, instead of splitting only at the first colon.
- **Quicken for Mac `.qmtf` files** are accepted, including upper-case section headers, several accounts listed under one header, and blank account types.
- **Fixed:** a CSV with separate Debit and Credit columns no longer imports every amount as 0. The same Money file can no longer be imported twice. Transfer Detection Rules now come before Saved Mappings in the CSV step.

## Look and feel

Every screen was checked against one set of layout rules, so pages and dialogs now behave the same way throughout the app:

- Dialogs have one title style, and the explanation sits in an info tooltip beside the title instead of a paragraph shown every time the dialog opens.
- List filters use the same segmented control with counts, and table headers can be sorted from the keyboard.
- Reports keep their toolbar on screen while loading. Empty and error states look the same everywhere, and every error has **Try Again**.
- On a phone, a control that sits alone on a row fills the width.
- Refresh buttons show how old the data is on a second line.
- A transparent payee or institution logo that would disappear into the card gets a plain background.

## Security and correctness

Contributor-led audits of the whole codebase found and fixed these issues, each with a regression test:

- **Sign-in:** reuse of a stolen refresh token now revokes the whole session family. A delegate's account claim respects lockout and 2FA. An emergency-access claim is refused once the owner is back. Active 2FA can't be swapped out using only the password. A backup code can't be reused by two logins at the same moment.
- **SSO accounts** can no longer sign in with a leftover local password by default, because an SSO sign-in skips the app's 2FA. See `OIDC_ALLOW_PASSWORD_LOGIN` below for a fallback when your identity provider is down.
- **Email receipts:** a pasted "Forwarded message" header is trusted only when you forwarded the email yourself. The AI review card names who forwarded it.
- **Delegates:** the AI assistant offers a delegate only an approved list of read-only tools. Delegations and grants can only be created from the owner's own session.
- **The AI chat is cleared on logout**, so the next person on that browser tab doesn't see it.
- **Financial figures:** voided rows are left out of analytics. Refunds net within their category. A missing price or exchange rate shows as unknown, never as 0 or a 1:1 rate. Scheduled transactions auto-post on the user's date, not the server's. Investment period returns treat buys, sells and transfers as money moving in or out, not as performance. Monte Carlo and GEM compare in your own currency. Loan payment splits add up to the payment. A closed account can't receive rows through a future date or an import. A backup restore recalculates balances from the ledger.
- **Custom currencies** of a deleted user are deactivated instead of becoming system currencies for everyone.

## For operators

- **22 migrations**, all safe to roll back to 1.19.x:
  - New tables for warranties, shared expenses and payroll deduction rules.
  - New columns: SimpleFIN credentials and balance timestamps, `scheduled_transactions.reference_number`, and `import_jobs.content_sha256` (filled in for past jobs, unique per user).
  - New `RETURN_OF_CAPITAL` investment action.
  - `accounts.mortgage_type` is filled in from the legacy mortgage flags and defaults to `ANNUITY`. The flag columns stay, so 1.19.x still runs against this schema; they are dropped no earlier than 1.21.0.
  - Delegations that already granted the whole ledger get the new Warranties section, so their AI access is unchanged.
  - Delegation RLS policies now only accept writes from the owner's own session. A trigger deactivates a custom currency when its creator is deleted.
  - `20261028205727_oidc_users_drop_local_password.sql` is a no-op. Only a `main` build between #1913 and #1932 ran its old body, which cleared SSO users' local passwords; **Unlink SSO** recovers those accounts.
- **SSO password sign-in:** `OIDC_ALLOW_PASSWORD_LOGIN` (default `false`) lets an SSO account that still has a local password sign in with it, and keeps the app's 2FA on SSO logins. Without app 2FA that password is a single factor, so turn 2FA on for those accounts. Admins also get **Unlink SSO**, which turns an SSO account back into a local one with a temporary password.
- **Postgres JIT:** db-init now sets `jit = off` for the app role on every start. On Postgres builds with LLVM, the register's count query took about 15 s with JIT and about 30 ms without. Under CNPG `managed.roles`, where the owner has no CREATEROLE, it only logs a warning, so set it yourself there.
- **UnifiedPush:** `UNIFIEDPUSH_PRIVATE_ENDPOINTS` accepts 100.64.0.0/10 (CGNAT, Tailscale) and 198.18.0.0/15. An endpoint that isn't pinned to an address now refuses DNS answers that point to private addresses. The AI base URL check now refuses those two ranges.
- **SimpleFIN** needs nothing configured on the server beyond the existing `ENCRYPTION_KEY`.
- **Container images** in the Dockerfiles and the E2E compose file are pinned by digest, and a guard keeps them in step with `docs/container-images.md`.
- **API:** `POST /transactions/:id/convert-to-transfer` and `/convert-to-transaction`, the `/payroll-deductions` routes (including `GET /payroll-deductions/report?year=`), and `POST /admin/users/:id/unlink-sso`. When there is no daily price move, the investment report's "today's change" is now `null`.
