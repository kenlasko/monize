# v1.22.0

Estimated amounts on imported cross-currency transfers are now recorded properly, marked on the row, flagged in every figure that includes one, and confirmable in one click. Categories gain a third type, **Transfer**, for what a movement between your own accounts was for.

## Transactions and import

- **Estimated amounts:** when an imported file holds a transfer into an account in another currency, Monize creates that account's side before its own file arrives. Its amount is now estimated at the exchange rate of the transfer's date, instead of copying the other side's figure, so 100 USD no longer becomes +100 EUR. If no rate is available for that pair and date, the row is not imported and the import result names them.
- **Estimated amount badge:** such a row shows an **Estimated amount** badge in every register layout. The state no longer lives in the description, so editing or clearing the memo cannot change it, and the old `PENDING IMPORT` text is removed from existing descriptions.
- **Confirm amount:** choose **Confirm amount** on a row to keep its estimate as the actual amount. Importing the other account's file, or editing the transfer to state a different amount, confirms it too. Confirming can be undone from the action history.
- **Find them:** search transactions for `is:provisional` (or `-is:provisional` for the rest).

## Reports and balances

- **Estimates are marked, not hidden:** account balances, net worth, custom reports, transaction analytics, the Cash Flow, Monthly Category Breakdown and Income vs Expenses reports, the payee and category panels, budget transfer actuals, the currency detail page, the AI assistant and MCP keep the estimate in their totals and say how many estimated amounts a figure includes.
- **No artificial gain:** a cross-currency transfer with one confirmed side and one estimated side now nets to zero in the reporting currency, rather than showing a cash movement that never happened.

## Transfer categories

Categories now have three types: Income, Expense and **Transfer**. Nothing that exists changes: your categories, the categories already on your transfers, and your saved reports, rules, scheduled transactions and budgets stay as they are, and every report and budget shows the figures it showed before until you use a Transfer category. A transfer may still hold an Income or Expense category, and keeps counting as it did.

- **Transfer categories:** create one on the Categories page (**Type: Transfer**); the default category import (offered only while you have no categories) now also creates a short **Transfers** set; existing categories are not touched. A Transfer category goes only on a transfer, so an ordinary transaction or a split's category line does not take one.
- **One category per transfer:** setting, changing or removing the category on either side of a transfer does the same to the other side. For a transfer to another person's account, each person keeps their own.
- **Splits:** a split's transfer line can carry a category, like a transfer entered on its own.
- **Monthly & Yearly Breakdown:** a transfer with a Transfer category is listed under it in the **Transfers** group, as an outgoing and an incoming row. Every other transfer stays in its per-account row.
- **Custom reports:** filter and group by Transfer category, and measure transfers as **Outgoing**, **Incoming** or **Net**.
- **Budgets:** a budget line can name a Transfer category and wins over a per-account transfer line for the transfers that hold it. Budget income is shown as **Actual Income**, **Transfer Funding** and **Total Budget Funding**; the total is the figure shown before.
- **Rules, imports, the assistant and MCP:** a rule can set a category of any type on a transfer; a payee's default category is never applied to a transfer; a Transfer category in an import file is imported only onto a transfer, and the import says how many rows were left uncategorized.
