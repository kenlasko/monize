# v1.21.0

Crypto wallets, the user guide inside the app, a redesigned dashboard you arrange in place, a command palette, a mobile tab bar, and new options for bills: a Custom frequency, estimated amounts and mortgage fees. Reports now convert past amounts at the exchange rate of their own date.

## Crypto

- **Crypto wallets:** a new **Crypto** account type holds one coin and works like a chequing account, with transactions, transfers, bills, imports and rules. A transfer that pays a network fee is a split with a fee line.
- **Crypto currencies:** when adding a currency, choose **Fiat Currency** or **Cryptocurrency**. A coin can be priced from a Yahoo pair such as `BTC-EUR`, and coin rates refresh on weekends too.
- **Eight decimal places:** amounts in a coin keep up to eight places, so small fees are no longer stored as zero. Ordinary currencies round exactly as before.
- **Fixed:** crypto held as a security (for example `BTC-EUR` in an investment account) now gets its price in the right currency.

## Help inside the app

- **The user guide opens in a side panel** from the **?** on each page, with screenshots in your current theme. It works offline, and the panel can be resized and has working Back and Forward buttons.
- **Learn more** links on tooltips, empty screens and error messages open the exact section that explains them.
- **Search the guide** with a bare **?** from any screen.
- **A Help menu** in the header has help for the current page, keyboard shortcuts, the page's tour and What's New.
- **The AI assistant and MCP** answer "how do I..." questions from the guide and cite the sections they used.

## Dashboard

- **A new hero** at the top greets you and shows your net worth or portfolio value with a chart. On **Auto** (the default) it switches between the two every minute; the dots beside the greeting switch it yourself.
- **Arrange the dashboard in place:** the gear puts the page in edit mode, where you drag widgets, resize them, and add or remove them. Nothing is saved until you press **Done**.
- **Trivia widget:** a daily fact about your net worth, investments, spending and warranties. Off by default; add it in edit mode. With an AI provider, switch on **AI trivia** in AI Settings for more varied wording. Your figures and account names are never sent to the AI.
- **Colour-coded widgets** by area (accounts, investments, bills, insights), and widgets such as Favourite Accounts show institution logos.
- **Faster:** the hero no longer waits for the portfolio figures before it appears.

## Getting around

- **Command palette:** **Ctrl/Cmd+K** searches accounts, payees, categories, bills, securities, reports, pages, settings, transactions and the guide in one box. **Ctrl/Cmd+Enter** starts a new transaction for the best-matching payee.
- **Mobile tab bar:** on phones and tablets a bottom bar replaces the menu, with three tabs you choose in Settings -> Preferences, a **+** button and **More**. The **+** button adds whatever the page is about, such as a new account on Accounts.
- **Look and feel:** a split-screen sign-in page, an animated loading screen, refreshed charts and cards, and dialogs that use the full height of the window before they scroll. Each form dialog now opens at the same size wherever it is opened.

## Bills & Deposits and loans

- **Custom frequency:** repeat on chosen weekdays, days of the month (including the last day) or dates of the year, every 1-12 weeks, months or years.
- **Estimated amounts:** switch on **Estimate** and each occurrence's amount is worked out from your payment history to that payee, allowing for seasonal patterns. Estimated bills are not auto-posted.
- **Mortgage fees:** add insurance, taxes or other fees to a mortgage payment, as a fixed amount or a percentage of the balance, each with its own category. Each fee becomes its own line on the scheduled payment.

## Investments

- **Price from a web page:** a security can take its prices from a table on a web page you name, for quotes Yahoo and MSN don't carry. Use **Preview** to check the page before saving.
- **US Treasury securities:** bills, notes, bonds, TIPS and FRNs join the government bond support.
- **Bond interest** can be recorded against its bond, so the coupon shows under that bond.
- **Investment Performance report:** Gain/Loss and Return now cover the period you select, matching the Investments page, and a new **% Change** column shows the price change.
- **Time-weighted return chart** in Investment Performance -> Performance, for the whole portfolio, each account or each security.
- **Faster entry:** the investment transaction form is wider, the Security field is searchable with **+ Create**, and Quantity, Price and Commission share one line.

## Reports

- **Past amounts use past exchange rates:** reports, budgets and portfolio cost basis convert each amount at the rate for its own date, not today's. When no rate is stored for that date, the report names the currency instead of guessing.
- **Custom reports:** clicking a row, category, payee, tag or period opens Transactions with the report's own filters applied. A list showing both income and expenses now keeps each amount's sign and totals to a net amount.
- **Monthly Breakdown:** fold the Income, Expenses and Transfers groups, a **Summary only** switch, and categories with no subcategories on a single line.

## Transactions and import

- **Cross-currency transfers inside a split:** a split's transfer line to an account in another currency shows the amount received, filled in from the day's rate and editable.
- **CSV import reports what it can't read:** a row with a date or amount it can't read is listed with its line number, not dropped or stored as zero. The date format is now detected from every row, and amounts with trailing signs, `DR`/`CR`, parentheses or currency labels are understood.
- **Fixed:** removing a split line through the API now lowers the transaction's total, and keeps the transfer in the other account.

## Other improvements

- **Payroll deductions:** each period keeps its own maximum and expected amount, so entering next year's maximum no longer changes this year's figures.
- **Email receipts:** Save explains what is missing and jumps to it, a pasted sender address keeps only its domain, and the search and date fields are wider.
- **AI assistant and MCP:** new tools to list and manage tags, and to add or remove tags on a transaction or a single split line.
- **Warranties:** **Add Existing** copies a document you already uploaded onto a warranty.
- **Shared Expenses:** the People tab is now a table showing each person's linked payee and groups.

## Fixes

- Merging two payees keeps the merged payee's warranties, email receipt profiles, loan overpayment links and shared-expense links.
- An account that bills or transfers still use can no longer be deleted (which silently removed those bills); close it instead.
- Restoring a voided transfer as Cleared or Reconciled no longer marks the other account's side the same way.
- With **Lock reconciled transactions** on, voiding one side of a transfer no longer voids a reconciled other side.
- Undo, import and budgets now use your own time zone's day, so balances and the current budget period are right late in the evening.
- **Add Split** works again when Monize is opened over plain HTTP on a local network.
- The Currencies page shows a symbol for currencies saved without one.
- A security audit of this release's changes fixed several ways a crafted CSV file or web page could slow down or overload the server.

## For operators

- **15 migrations.** Most are additive (new tables for mortgage fees, per-period payroll deduction figures and dashboard trivia; new columns for quote URLs, currency kind, estimated and custom-frequency schedules; the `CRYPTO` account type).
  - **Plan for a pause:** `20261106012628_money_scale_8.sql` widens 50 money columns from `NUMERIC(20,4)` to `NUMERIC(24,8)`. PostgreSQL rewrites each table under an exclusive lock, so reads and writes wait while it runs, longest on `transactions`. Take a backup first.
  - During a rolling upgrade, a 1.20.0 pod may show eight-place coin amounts rounded to four, or refuse to book a shared expense against one. Single-replica installs are unaffected.
  - Three new `SECURITY DEFINER` currency functions are granted to the app role by db-init.
- **No new environment variables.**
- **New outbound requests:** the web page quote provider fetches the URLs users enter. Only public addresses are allowed, with at most 3 redirects and a 5 MB limit. US Treasury data comes from TreasuryDirect and the BLS, and coin rates from Yahoo.
- **CSV and QIF:** a row with five or more decimal places on an ordinary-currency account is now refused instead of rounded.
- **API:** new `GET /portfolio/valuation`, `GET /net-worth/investments-period-result/securities`, `GET /net-worth/investments-twr-series`, the `/help` routes (`search`, `pages/:slug`, `images/:name`) and `POST /warranties/:id/attachments/copy`. `DELETE /transactions/:id/splits/:splitId` now re-totals the parent.
- Docker images now convert the guide's screenshots to WebP at build time.
